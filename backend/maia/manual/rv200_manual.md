# RV-200 Regional Railcar — Maintenance Manual (FICTIONAL)

This manual is entirely fictional. It was written for the MAIA research prototype and does not describe any real vehicle, manufacturer or operator.

## SAF-01 General safety rules

Before any work under or on the vehicle, the technician must apply lockout/tagout: isolate the traction supply, lower the pantograph, apply the parking brake, and hang a personal lock and tag on the isolation switch. Lockout/tagout must never be skipped, even for a quick visual check. Personal protective equipment is mandatory: safety shoes, high-visibility vest, gloves and safety glasses. Work in the inspection pit requires a second person within calling distance. If any safety condition cannot be met, stop work and inform the shift supervisor.

## SAF-02 Escalation rules

A reading below the critical limit of any procedure means the vehicle must be withdrawn from service. The technician must stop the procedure, tag the vehicle "OUT OF SERVICE" and notify the shift supervisor immediately. Readings between the minimum limit and the warning limit require a work order with priority "medium" within 7 days. Readings below the minimum limit and above the critical limit require a work order with priority "high" before the next service run. Never estimate or guess a limit value; use only the values in this manual.

## PROC-BRK-01 Brake pad inspection (bogie)

Applies to: bogies B1 and B2 of every RV-200 vehicle. Interval: weekly. Tools: brake pad thickness gauge, torch.
Step 1: Apply lockout/tagout according to SAF-01.
Step 2: Release the parking brake on the bogie under inspection so the pads can be inspected without load.
Step 3: Visually inspect the left and right brake pads for cracks, oil contamination or missing retaining pins.
Step 4: Measure the friction material thickness of the left pad and the right pad at the thinnest point, in millimetres.
Step 5: Compare readings with the limits: new pad thickness 20 mm; warning limit 7 mm; minimum limit 5 mm; critical limit 3 mm.
Step 6: Re-apply the parking brake and remove lockout/tagout only when all tools are clear.
Pads are always replaced in pairs on the same axle (left and right). Replacement part: brake pad, part number BP-2207 (one unit per pad, order 2 per axle).

## PROC-WHL-02 Wheel flange inspection

Applies to: all wheelsets. Interval: monthly. Tools: wheel profile gauge WG-01.
Step 1: Apply lockout/tagout according to SAF-01.
Step 2: Clean the flange area of the wheel to be measured.
Step 3: Measure flange thickness with gauge WG-01, in millimetres.
Step 4: Measure flange height, in millimetres.
Step 5: Compare with limits: flange thickness minimum 26 mm, warning 27.5 mm, critical 24 mm, maximum 33 mm; flange height maximum 36 mm.
Step 6: Remove lockout/tagout.
A flange below the minimum requires wheelset reprofiling; raise a work order for the wheel lathe. There is no spare part for this procedure.

## PROC-PAN-03 Pantograph carbon strip inspection

Applies to: roof pantograph. Interval: weekly. Tools: roof access platform, calliper.
Step 1: Apply lockout/tagout according to SAF-01 and confirm the pantograph is lowered and earthed.
Step 2: Access the roof using the access platform with fall protection.
Step 3: Inspect the carbon strip for cracks, chips or uneven wear.
Step 4: Measure remaining carbon thickness at the thinnest point, in millimetres.
Step 5: Compare with limits: new strip 22 mm; warning limit 8 mm; minimum limit 5 mm; critical limit 3 mm.
Step 6: Leave the roof, remove lockout/tagout.
Replacement part: carbon strip, part number CS-1130 (one per pantograph).

## PROC-BAT-04 Battery box check

Applies to: 110 V auxiliary battery box. Interval: monthly. Tools: multimeter, insulated gloves.
Step 1: Apply lockout/tagout according to SAF-01.
Step 2: Open the battery box and inspect for electrolyte leaks, corrosion and loose terminals.
Step 3: Measure standby voltage across the main terminals, in volts.
Step 4: Compare with limits: nominal 110 V; warning limit 100 V; minimum limit 96 V; critical limit 90 V.
Step 5: Inspect the main fuse. Replacement fuse: part number BF-125.
Step 6: Close the battery box and remove lockout/tagout.

## PROC-DOR-05 Passenger door function test

Applies to: all passenger doors. Interval: weekly. Tools: stopwatch, force gauge.
Step 1: Switch the vehicle to maintenance mode (lockout/tagout is not required for this functional test, but traction must be isolated).
Step 2: Open and close the door three times and time the opening cycle, in seconds.
Step 3: Compare with limits: opening time between 2.5 s and 3.5 s.
Step 4: Test obstacle detection with the force gauge; the door must reverse at a force of 150 N or less.
Step 5: If obstacle detection fails, the door must be locked out of use and the door sensor replaced.
Replacement part: door obstacle sensor, part number DS-450.

## GLO-01 Glossary

Bogie: the wheeled frame under each end of the vehicle (B1 front, B2 rear). Work order: a request to carry out corrective maintenance, with a priority (low, medium, high) and the parts needed. Shift report: a summary of all steps, readings and work orders created during a shift, sent to the supervisor.
